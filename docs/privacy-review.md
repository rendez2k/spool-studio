# Privacy notice: operator review before publishing

The factual data-flow description is based on this application’s implementation, not a general-purpose policy template. `out/privacy.html` remains visibly labelled as a draft. Do not remove its review markers or publish it as a final legal notice until the following are resolved.

- Confirm the individual operator’s full legal name. ProductKit Digital is the supplied contact brand, not a verified registered company. The user has chosen an individual operator, but has not yet supplied the exact name.
- Confirm the proposed purpose-to-lawful-basis mapping with appropriate advice: service provision for account/library functions, legitimate interests for security/support, and legal obligations where applicable. Document any required legitimate-interest assessment; do not treat using the app as blanket consent.
- Check the current Netlify hosting/database, Clerk authentication and relevant Cloudflare DNS agreements and processing arrangements, including deployment/support access locations, international transfers and safeguards, and how users can obtain details. Review legacy Sites processing separately where retained data remains there. General supplier privacy policies alone do not establish the operator’s contractual safeguards.
- Identify the mailbox provider for hello@productkit.digital and approve retention criteria for support correspondence. Confirm hosting logs and backup retention and deletion behaviour. Do not promise invented time limits or UK-only hosting.
- Establish an account-verification, export and deletion procedure. Inventory and phone-batch rows are separate; deletion must cover both, including import fingerprints. There is no self-service delete endpoint or automatic inactivity purge. “Used” and “Clear this phone” are not server deletion.
- Confirm contact-mailbox monitoring. Do not request passwords, sign-in codes or unnecessary identity documents. Do not add a response-time guarantee unless it can be met.
- Remove draft/review copy only after replacing it with confirmed wording. Update the notice date and re-run the route/content tests. Obtain explicit approval before deploying to the public Spool Studio audience.

Contact and privacy pages are deliberately available without signing in. They introduce no analytics, remote fonts, embeds or contact-form backend. Keep Site hosting bindings, real environment files and private inventory out of Git. The public notice also describes the optional server-side product lookup and on-device barcode flow; check that these descriptions stay aligned with the implementation.

Reference checked 9 September 2026: [ICO: privacy information to provide](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/the-right-to-be-informed/what-privacy-information-should-we-provide/). Legal review is still needed; the implementation and this checklist are not a claim of GDPR compliance.
