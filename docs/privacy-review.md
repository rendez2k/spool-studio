## Backup evidence checked 10 September 2026

The production site's read-only Netlify metadata reports Pro (`nf_team_pro`). Its snapshot endpoint returned ten snapshots, with creation dates but no expiry field. No database data was read, restored or deleted for this check.

[Netlify's current backup documentation](https://docs.netlify.com/build/data-and-storage/netlify-database/backup-and-recovery/) distinguishes scheduled backups from publish-triggered backups. Its credit-based Pro policy lists 30 days for scheduled copies and the last ten deploy backups for publish-triggered copies. The latter is count-based, not a maximum number of days. Confirm the applicable billing terms and any other retained copies before turning this into a public retention promise.

A database restore can reintroduce erased content and old bridge credentials. Before any restore, preserve the verified erasure/revocation history outside the backup being restored, keep application access closed, and reapply those changes before reopening. This is an operator recovery requirement, not an automated capability of the current erasure endpoint. Do not restore production merely to test this procedure.

## Support correspondence procedure approved in principle

Keep ordinary support correspondence while a case is open and for 12 months after closure, to handle follow-up questions and recurring faults. Record the closure date. Review due cases regularly, delete the conversation and unnecessary attachments from working mail folders, and check sent/trash copies. Record any justified extension for an unresolved legal/dispute requirement, including a review date. Do not apply this schedule indiscriminately to invoices, formal rights-request records or other records with a separate justified requirement.

No mailbox was opened, changed or automatically purged during setup. Provider deletion/backups, monitoring responsibility and operational scheduling still need confirmation. The ICO does not prescribe a universal support-email period; this is the operator's selected purpose-based schedule, not a statutory 12-month rule. Reference: [ICO storage limitation](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/storage-limitation/).
