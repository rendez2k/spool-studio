Moving from the old app?

  1. Open spool-studio.uk/app.html in Android Chrome and sign in.
  2. Install Spool Studio from that address. An app installed from the old ChatGPT site cannot switch domains in place.
  3. Keep the old app until your rolls have been migrated and checked. Then remove its old shortcut or uninstall it from Android App info.

Reinstalling does not move your old library or locally loaded 3MF files. NFC writing still needs Android Chrome, NFC enabled and a compatible tag. Check NFC setup.

What works on your phone?

  • Android Chrome: library, imports and barcode camera scanning, plus NFC writing with NFC enabled and a compatible tag.
  • iPhone / iPad: use your library and imports, and scan barcodes where camera access is available. This web app cannot write NFC tags on iOS. Installing it does not change that.
  • Desktop: organise your shelf, match 3MF projects and print labels. Open the saved programmer page on Android for NFC.

Barcode scanning asks for camera permission and has a typed-code fallback. Images are decoded on your device. Adding new filament · NFC prerequisites.
