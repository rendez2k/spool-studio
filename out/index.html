Matching options & how it works

Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer projects, up to 100 MB each and 8 files at a time. Imports stay in this browser. Load your own 3MF to compare its colours.

Matte and standard PLA stay separate; other materials and PLA+ are not silently substituted. The entire available library is compared, ignoring collection filters and excluding used-up rolls.

Saved slots may be unused; untick those you do not need. Previews are saved images, not rotatable 3D models, and do not change when you choose alternatives. This does not read painted regions or sliced usage, reserve stock, or confirm remaining grams.

Colour matches use approximate library swatches. “Close” is a broad CIEDE2000 ΔE00 ≤ 15 shortlist, not a colour guarantee. Only a saved named shade, brand and profile can confirm identity. Packaging describes the purchase, not whether you have since mounted a refill.

Review your replacement filaments

    Only included slots change. Excluded slots, geometry, painting and slot numbering remain intact—even if you choose the same spool twice. Counts are not reserved and remaining weight is not checked.

    This is an editable project, not a ready-to-print file. Library entries are not calibrated slicer presets. New slot labels identify your chosen stock; original temperatures, flow, speeds, purge volumes and printer settings remain for review. Select suitable profiles and your actual printer, recalculate purging and re-slice before printing. Saved preview images still show the original colours.

    The original file is untouched. Pre-sliced projects and embedded filament preset bundles are rejected rather than silently changed.
