About your filament tag

Use Android Chrome with NFC enabled, Paxx U1 OpenSpool support and a writable NTAG215/216. Hold one tag at a time.

Write & verify writes once, then listens for a fresh read. If prompted, lift and tap the same tag again—no extra button. An interrupted check does not mean the write failed.

The tag uses Generic material · Basic subtype. Matte/silk finish stays in your library; no calibrated slicer profile or remaining weight is installed.

Phone verification checks tag contents, not printer recognition. Check the U1’s detected material, colour and loading temperature. No tag is locked and no printer settings are changed remotely.

Temperatures are optional for Paxx, but SpoolPainter expects a minimum and maximum. Use the actual spool’s recommended range.

Your approval lasts for this batch during this visit. A new batch needs a fresh approval; every tag still needs a Write & verify tap.
