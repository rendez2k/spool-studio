Local bridge setup

A computer on your printer’s network must keep the bridge running. It makes outbound connections only; do not forward printer ports or expose Moonraker to the internet.

This printer key is separate from your weight-sync key. Replacing or revoking it stops new requests, but cannot recall a command already sent to the printer.

Contains a secret key and enables reviewed metadata writes. Keep it private; never commit it to GitHub.

In your local Spool Studio repository, using Node.js 22 or newer:

npm ci
node scripts/printer-bridge.mjs "path/to/spool-studio-printer.private.json" --check
node scripts/printer-bridge.mjs "path/to/spool-studio-printer.private.json"

--check is read-only. The second command waits for your reviewed app requests. Stop it with Ctrl+C.
